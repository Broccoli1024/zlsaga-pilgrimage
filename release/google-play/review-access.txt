App access: Some functionality is restricted by sign-in.

Use a dedicated ordinary-user account. Enter its email and password only in
the Play Console app access credential fields, not in this file or Git.

Reviewer instructions:
Open the navigation menu and select Login. Sign in using the supplied review
account. The map and published spot details are accessible without signing in.
After signing in, you can record visits, save routes, and submit photos from
the bottom of a spot detail page. Photos are pending until the operator approves
them. Published photos can be reported and their authors can be blocked.
Blocked authors can be managed in My Page. Account deletion is also available
in My Page, and information is available at https://pilgrimapp.jp/delete-account.
No purchase is required. The account does not require an authenticator code.
The administrator page is a separate operator-only tool; it is not a consumer
feature, and the review account has no administrator privileges.

Before submission:
- Confirm the review email and verify login in the actual Android release.
- Keep the account active and available during review.
- Use a separate account when testing deletion so review credentials remain valid.
- Do not put personal photos, personal visit history, or admin permissions here.
